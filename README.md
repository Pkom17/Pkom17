## Pascal Komena

Full-stack engineer and health information systems specialist, based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire.
*Ingénieur full-stack et spécialiste des systèmes d'information de santé, basé à Abidjan.*

For 8+ years I have built and run national-scale health information systems in Côte d'Ivoire: laboratory information systems, electronic medical records and the interoperability layer that connects them.

- **OpenELIS Global**: national laboratory information system, deployed in 170+ laboratory sites
- **SIGDEP-3 / OpenMRS**: HIV patient records, consolidated national server
- **Interoperability**: OpenHIE architecture with OpenHIM and HAPI FHIR
- **National HMIS Technical Working Group** member

### Featured work

- **[Community monitoring platform for health and education](https://github.com/Pkom17/case-study-community-monitoring)**: data pipeline from KoboToolbox to PostgreSQL and Superset, Strapi back office with an append-only history, privacy rules enforced at extraction time, 163 automated tests. *(case study)*
